    Bio

My specialities include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React JS. I'm currently pursuing a bachelors degree in Computer Science at University of Ilorin, Nigeria (Better by Far). I also have a background in teaching and I Love cats.

I'm here to learn not just the curriculum, but also how to think critically, communicate effectively, and contribute meaningfully to any team or project I'm part of.
